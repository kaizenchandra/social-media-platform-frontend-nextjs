**Updated:** 2026-10-09. **Current phase:** 12 — Advanced Social Platform Features.

## Current truth

Common has a responsive design system and application shell, typed account/authentication interfaces, audience-filtered profiles/social connections, permission-filtered feeds/engagement, and private content creation with simulated upload/safety lifecycle interfaces. Authentication is enabled only when NODE_ENV=development and COMMON_AUTH_MODE=mock. Production always fails closed, including when the mock flag or forged preview cookies are supplied. No identity provider, email delivery, WebAuthn registration/assertion, real device inventory, or account-erasure service is connected. Phase 01 was interrupted; its entire foundation exit gate is not claimed complete. Personal and group messaging now have tab-local synthetic previews. Rich-media viewing fixtures and a separate unavailable live provider UI are now implemented. Phases 10 and 11 now add permission-filtered communities/discovery and simulated trust workflows. Phase 12 adds creator management, private schedule plans, marketplace fixtures/private drafts, assistance provider boundaries and well-being controls. No phase after 12 was started.
